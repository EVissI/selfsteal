About

I am a writer based between Lisbon and the internet. For the past decade I have written essays and reported features for a handful of magazines, and these days most of my work lands here first.

Before writing full time I worked as an editor and, briefly and badly, as a software engineer — which is probably why so much of what I write circles back to the tools we use and the attention they ask of us.
